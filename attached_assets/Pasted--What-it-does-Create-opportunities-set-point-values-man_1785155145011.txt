  What it does: Create opportunities, set point values,
                manage employee plans, upload insurance
                files, view reports and analytics

The "implementor" is the person or company that deploys CareReward for a group of users. In most cases this is: 

CareReward Internal Team    → Super admin, full control
Employer HR Administrator   → Manages their employees
Insurance Company Rep       → Defines clinical opportunities


"View for Opp Build"
This means a screen inside the Implementor Portal where someone can create and configure opportunities. Right now your opportunities are hard-coded in the seed file. The "opp build" view makes them dynamic — anyone with the right permissions can create new ones without a developer.

"Who Defines the Opp + Logic and Also Assigns Points"
This is a roles and permissions question. The answer depends on our business model but here is the most logical structure for CareReward:
ROLE 1 — CareReward Super Admin
  Who:    Your internal team
  Can do: Everything. Create master opportunity catalog,
          set global point values, manage all employers,
          override anything.

ROLE 2 — Employer Admin / Implementor
  Who:    HR manager at Acme Corp for example
  Can do: Choose which opportunities from the master
          catalog apply to their employees.
          Set custom point multipliers for their plan.
          Upload insurance implementation file.
          View their employees' engagement reports.
          Cannot create new opportunity types —
          only picks from what CareReward defined.

ROLE 3 — Insurance Company Rep
  Who:    Partner insurance companies
  Can do: Define clinical protocol opportunities
          (e.g. "Get your A1C tested if diabetic")
          that are medically validated.
          Set point values tied to their cost savings.

ROLE 4 — End User (Mobile App)
  Who:    The employee/member
  Can do: View assigned opportunities only.
          Complete them.
          Cannot see who defined them or why.
How the Three Pieces Connect
Here is the full flow from opportunity creation to a user earning points:
STEP 1 — DEFINE (Super Admin or Insurance Rep)
  Someone with admin access logs into the
  Implementor Portal and creates an opportunity
  using the "opp build" view.
  Sets title, category, points value, logic.
  Saves to the opportunities table in your DB.

STEP 2 — CONFIGURE (Employer Admin / Implementor)
  Employer HR logs into their portal view.
  Sees the master catalog of opportunities.
  Selects which ones apply to their employees.
  Can adjust point values within allowed range.
  This creates employer_opportunity_configs records.

STEP 3 — ASSIGN (Nightly Rules Engine — already built)
  The scheduler job you already have runs at 2AM.
  For each user it checks:
    - What employer are they with?
    - Which opportunities did that employer activate?
    - Has this user already been assigned this?
    - Are they eligible based on their health profile?
  Creates userOpportunities rows for eligible users.

STEP 4 — COMPLETE (End User — Mobile App)
  User sees the opportunity on their phone.
  Completes the required action.
  App calls POST /api/opportunities/complete.
  Points are added to their balance.

STEP 5 — REPORT (Employer Admin / Implementor)
  Employer logs into portal.
  Sees dashboard: how many employees completed,
  total points awarded, cost savings estimate.
